# Test piano pieces: sources and licences

Real piano performances as MIDI, played by the test piano (stream_service/piano_replay.py) into every screen's
sound view the way the Steinway's MIDI arrives. Any `.mid` dropped here appears in the Room page's Test music and
the console's Test piano, labelled by its file name. Private, non-commercial use in this house only; never
redistributed.

## Classical: MAESTRO v3.0.0 (Google Magenta)

Virtuosic performances recorded on Yamaha Disklavier concert grands at the International Piano-e-Competition
(2004–2018), with key velocities and pedals. Licence: **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**.
Source: https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/maestro (maestro-v3.0.0-midi.zip, sha256 70470ee2…de12c).
Citation: Curtis Hawthorne et al., "Enabling Factorized Piano Music Modeling and Generation with the MAESTRO
Dataset", ICLR 2019.

The ten `Classical - …` files are taken unchanged; only renamed.

## Jazz: PiJAMA (Queen Mary University of London)

Solo jazz piano performances, transcribed from commercial recordings to MIDI by the Kong et al. high-resolution
piano transcription model (notes and sustain pedal). Licence of the MIDI release: **CC BY-NC 4.0**
(https://zenodo.org/records/8354955, midi_kong.zip, md5 9920ab88ec11757ca0c138478ef27653).
Citation: Drew Edwards, Simon Dixon, Emmanouil Benetos, "PiJAMA: Piano Jazz with Automatic MIDI Annotations",
TISMIR 6(1), 2023. The compositions and the original recordings remain their owners'.

The fifteen `Jazz - …` files are taken unchanged; only renamed.

The built-in "Test pattern: …" entries are generated by piano_replay.py (scales, chords, every key) for checking
mappings, not music.
