Thin teal: the DSP's energy (music.features). Thick teal: the energy the screen's scene gets. Blue: its activity. Amber spikes: its beat signal; amber ticks on top: the DSP clock's beats. A signal "follows the DSP" when it moves with the DSP's energy (r ≥ 0.6 within 1.6 s) and by at least a quarter as much.
