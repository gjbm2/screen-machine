- Qwen-Image-2.1's Qwen Research licence has no acceptable-use policy, no content-filter requirement and no anti-circumvention clause. Its only real limits are 'research or evaluation purposes only', export control, 'Built with Qwen' attribution, and PRC law with Hangzhou courts. FLUX Kommunity, FLUX.2 Non-Commercial and Ideogram all expressly list 'private entertainment' and 'hobby projects' as non-commercial use. Qwen-Image-2.1 says only 'research or evaluation', which does not obviously cover a home gallery. Source: the FLUX Kommunity §1(c) and Ideogram §1(d) LICENSE.md files on HF.

Safety-bypass LoRAs and uncensoring fine-tunes (e.g. Kroma on Krea 2) are therefore arguably a licence breach, not just a technical step. ### new findings
- LTX-2.x licence (Attachment A) incorporates the Lightricks Acceptable Use Policy by reference: https://static.lightricks.com/legal/ltx-acceptable-use-policy.pdf, 'Last Updated: March 30, 2026'. The licence says Lightricks may update it and 'the version in effect at the time of your use governs'. Round-1 (r-video.md, r-expression.md) missed it. It bars sexually explicit content (sex acts, fetishes), graphic violence/gratuitous gore, violence 'inspired by real acts of violence', sexual violence, shaming/humiliating individuals, impersonating real entities without consent, and bypassing product restrictions. It also says Lightricks trains its models 'to refuse harmful instructions'. This bears directly on the owner's violence and political-satire requirements; plain nudity is not named.
- LTX-2.x licence also reserves the right to 'restrict (remotely or otherwise) usage', 'modify the Output of LTX-2.x based on updates', and asks licensees to use the latest version. It forbids removing latent-disclosure or watermark features 'in furtherance of AI Regulations' (EU AI Act, California AI Transparency Act); breach allows immediate revocation. Source: https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2/blob/main/LICENSE-2_x (last changed 2026-10-13, which only added a licensing email).
- The ComfyUI LTX-2.5 FLF2V template (templates/video_ltx2_5_flf2v.json, added 2026-08-11) is NOT a 4K/DFR graph. It defaults to 1280x720 at 24 fps, uses LTXVAddGuide at frames 0 and -1, and has no temporal upscaler. The only ComfyUI DFR template (video_ltx2_5_dfr_i2v.json) was added 2026-10-13 and is i2v-only (1280x704 default plus x2 temporal to 48 fps). Native 3840x2176 first-last-frame therefore means the ltx-pipelines CLI DFRPipeline with two '--image PATH FRAME_IDX STRENGTH' conditions (dfr_pipeline.py accepts an images list), or a hand-adapted ComfyUI graph. Source: https://github.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates
